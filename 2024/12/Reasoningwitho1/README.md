# Reasoning with o1

![V2_DeepLearning_ Reasoning_with_o1_Banner_2070x1080](https://info.deeplearning.ai/hs-fs/hubfs/V2_DeepLearning_%20Reasoning_with_o1_Banner_2070x1080.png)

Dear learner, 

 

Learn how to use OpenAI’s o1 model to tackle advanced reasoning tasks like coding, planning, and image understanding in [Reasoning with o1](https://learn.deeplearning.ai/courses/reasoning-with-o1/), a free course made in collaboration with OpenAI, and taught by Colin Jarvis, its Head of AI Solutions.

 

You’ll explore:

- When and how to use the o1 model for tasks requiring advanced reasoning.
- Effective prompting strategies, including meta-prompting, to optimize results.
- Practical applications like coding, vision tasks, and building workflows that balance intelligence and cost.


![Launch email GIFs (61)](https://info.deeplearning.ai/hs-fs/hubfs/Launch%20email%20GIFs%20(61).gif)

Learn how o1’s unique features, such as chain-of-thought prompting and test-time compute, enable it to reason through complex problems by breaking them into manageable steps. With hands-on examples and guidance, this course equips you to build applications that rely on abstract and complex reasoning.
